📖 How Your Order Works

Every order is reviewed by Nkem before it is made, so we can check your choices, materials, availability, and any special requests. ♡

🛍️

1. Choose

Pick your product, size, and the normal colour or choose another colour. Add any extra notes you want us to see.

💌

2. Send

Add your items to the cart, enter your customer details, then click Place Order & Email Nkem.

🔎

3. Nkem Reviews

I review your order, colour choices, notes, and material availability. If a requested colour is unavailable or needs extra materials, I will contact you first.

🧶

4. We Make It

Once everything is confirmed, your crochet item is made according to the approved order and prepared for delivery.

💗 About colour changes: Our products are designed using the colours and materials planned for the original design. Changing colours may mean using or purchasing yarn specifically for your order. Colour selections are reviewed with your order; any extra charge will be confirmed before proceeding. If a requested colour is unavailable, unusually expensive, or requires extra materials, an additional charge may be discussed after review. You will be told about any additional charge before we proceed.
✨ Need something completely different? Use Custom Orders to explain exactly what you want. You can even request a custom version of one of our existing products.
